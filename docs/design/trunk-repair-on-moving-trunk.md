# A red trunk proof is repaired even while newer landings keep touching the failing check's inputs

**Status:** Open for the owner's decision in [`OQ-TRM1`](#OQ-TRM1); **this document settles nothing itself**. Every option, cost comparison, ruling check, and leaning below is recorded from an operator request as **the designer's analysis and leaning, not an owner ruling** ([decision provenance review](../tillr/decision-provenance-review.md#6-how-this-stops-happening), [`OQ-PR21` and `OQ-PR22`](../tillr/decision-provenance-review.md#decision-ledger)); the owner may overturn any part of it. No Go code or configuration is changed in this commit.

> **In short.** On **2026-11-27** between `01:55` and `03:52`, the loop's own log and `.git/tillr-gate/trunk-repair.json` recorded **four** deferrals of a red trunk proof, each with `"repair deferred to proof of <tip>: newer commits touched a declared input"` across different landed files (`tools/tillr-run/native/fence.go`, `tools/tillr-drain/drain/item_dir_run_test.go`, `tools/tillr-drain/drain/item_worktrees_restart_ext_test.go`), the fourth also recording `"automatic revert not possible: trunk advanced beyond the proved commit"`, while the trunk grew from `4` to `20` landings past `refs/tillr/green` and stayed unproved for over two hours. Four coupled code paths in [`tools/tillr-drain`](../../tools/tillr-drain/) produce that loop: `go-test-tillr-drain` declares `22` repository-wide `reads` entries that `GatePassed` candidates never check under [`OQ-GP7`](./tillr-graph-is-the-path.md#decision-ledger); `HeavyRunner.offerRevert` refuses any revert the moment `main` advances past `run.TipSHA`; `RetainRedRepairs` defers fixer filing without proving a pass whenever any commit touches `check.Reads` (recording the unproved tip in `trunk-repair.json` as if repaired); and `priorDeferredChecks` inspects only the single most recent `heavy.json`, alternating `defer -> non-defer -> defer` without bound. Repair delay rises with trunk activity — the inverted polarity [the landing principles](./landing-principles.md) forbid.

- **Start at:** [Section 1](#1-the-2026-11-27-measurement-and-the-four-coupled-code-paths) for the 2026-11-27 measurement and exact root causes in `tools/tillr-drain`, [Section 2](#2-four-options-for-repairing-a-red-proof-on-a-moving-trunk) for the four options, [Section 3](#3-costs-checked-against-the-landing-principles-and-recorded-rulings) for the check against [landing principles](./landing-principles.md) and recorded owner rulings, and [`OQ-TRM1`](#OQ-TRM1) for the owner's choice.
- **Needs your ruling:** [`OQ-TRM1`](#OQ-TRM1).
- **Reads with:** [landing principles](./landing-principles.md), [the gate reference](../reference/gate.md) ([A red proof](../reference/gate.md#a-red-proof), [The trunk fixer's gate](../reference/gate.md#the-trunk-fixers-gate)), [the item graph design](./tillr-graph-is-the-path.md) ([Section 8.2](./tillr-graph-is-the-path.md#82-proof-and-a-red-trunk)), [fast-gate options for `tillr-drain`](./fast-gate-tillr-drain-options.md) ([`OQ-DFG1`](./fast-gate-tillr-drain-options.md#OQ-DFG1)), and [authority, not approval](../reference/authority-not-approval.md).

## 1. The 2026-11-27 measurement and the four coupled code paths

### 1.1 Measured deferrals on 2026-11-27 (`01:55`–`03:52`)

Measured on **2026-11-27** from the loop's own log and `.git/tillr-gate/trunk-repair.json` (carried verbatim in the filed request):

- Between **`01:55` and `03:52`** (`117` minutes), trunk repair was deferred **four times**.
- Every deferral recorded the reason `"repair deferred to proof of <tip>: newer commits touched a declared input"`, naming a different file each time, including [`tools/tillr-run/native/fence.go`](../../tools/tillr-run/native/fence.go), [`tools/tillr-drain/drain/item_dir_run_test.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/item_dir_run_test.go), and [`tools/tillr-drain/drain/item_worktrees_restart_ext_test.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/item_worktrees_restart_ext_test.go).
- The fourth deferral also recorded `"automatic revert not possible: trunk advanced beyond the proved commit"`.
- Over that window, the trunk went from **`4` to `20` landings** past the last proved commit (`refs/tillr/green`), and stayed unproved for **over two hours**.

Four mechanisms in [`tools/tillr-drain`](../../tools/tillr-drain/) and [`tools/gate/checks/go-test-tillr-drain.json`](../../tools/gate/checks/go-test-tillr-drain.json) combine to make that delay grow with trunk traffic.

### 1.2 `go-test-tillr-drain` declares 22 repository-wide `reads` entries, and `GatePassed` candidates never consult red heavy checks

1. **Broad declared inputs on `go-test-tillr-drain`.** Under owner rulings [`D-GR53`](../reference/gate.md#D-GR53) and [`D-GR56`](../reference/gate.md#D-GR56) ([`OQ-DFG1`](./fast-gate-tillr-drain-options.md#OQ-DFG1), built in commit `5e728e5a` via [`D-GR54`](../reference/gate.md#D-GR54)), `DeferredProofPackages` in [`tools/tillr-gate/goaffected/select.go`](../../tools/tillr-gate/goaffected/select.go) defers `github.com/mschulkind-oss/tillr-drain/drain` out of pre-merge `go-affected` into the after-merge `go-test-tillr-drain` proof. So result memory ([`D-GR8`](../reference/gate.md#why-its-this-way) in [`docs/reference/gate.md`](../reference/gate.md#remembered-passes)) never reuses a stale pass, [`tools/gate/checks/go-test-tillr-drain.json`](../../tools/gate/checks/go-test-tillr-drain.json) declares **`22` `reads` entries** covering all `11` `tools/tillr*` Go modules (`tools/tillr-cli/`, `tools/tillr-drain/`, `tools/tillr-gate/`, `tools/tillr-item/`, `tools/tillr-land/`, `tools/tillr-loop/`, `tools/tillr-roll/`, `tools/tillr-run/`, `tools/tillr-store/`, `tools/tillr-view/`, `tools/tillr/`), `config/tillr/*`, `tools/gate/go-test-edges.json`, `tools/go-test-durations.json`, and `yolo-packs/tillr/*`. Nearly every non-doc landing in the repository touches a declared input of `go-test-tillr-drain`.
2. **Declaration Go suites are not split into per-test checks.** In [`tools/tillr-drain/internal/gatespans/heavy_steps.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/internal/gatespans/heavy_steps.go), `RunHeavyLegacy` calls `GoSuiteFailures` ([`tools/tillr-drain/internal/gatespans/heavy_go.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/internal/gatespans/heavy_go.go)) on legacy `go-tool` failures to emit individual `go-test:<module>:<pkg>:<TestName>` checks with `Reads: nil` that replay in seconds via `JudgeHeavyGo`. By contrast, `JudgeHeavy` for declaration files (`tillr-gate checks --heavy-tree`) returns one monolithic `HeavyCheck{CheckID: "go-test-tillr-drain", Reads: meta.Reads}` — even though `ReadTriageBlock` and `ReadGoTestFailures` in [`tools/tillr-drain/internal/runhistory/refusal_cases.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/internal/runhistory/refusal_cases.go) already parse failing `(Package, Test)` cases from `tillr-gate`'s `.log` and `.triage.log` files. Every culprit-search replay (`FindFirstRed`) therefore re-runs the whole `go-test-tillr-drain` declaration (`6` shards), widening the window during which `main` moves.
3. **[`OQ-GP7`](./tillr-graph-is-the-path.md#decision-ledger) runs only on `GateFailed`, never on `GatePassed`.** In [`tools/tillr-drain/drain/loop.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/loop.go), `settleTrunkAlreadyRed` ([`tools/tillr-drain/drain/trunk.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/trunk.go)) is called only under `case GateFailed:`. Because an ordinary candidate's pre-merge gate does not run `tier: "heavy"` checks, a candidate touching `tools/tillr-run/native/fence.go` or `tools/tillr-drain/drain/*` answers `GatePassed` and lands directly into `go-test-tillr-drain`'s inputs while `go-test-tillr-drain` is already red.

### 1.3 `offerRevert` refuses whenever `main` moved by a single commit during the proof

In [`OQ-GP1`](./tillr-graph-is-the-path.md#decision-ledger) ([Section 8.2](./tillr-graph-is-the-path.md#82-proof-and-a-red-trunk), recorded in the gate reference as [`D-GR6`](../reference/gate.md#D-GR6)), the owner ruled that when bisection attributes a red check `K` to landing `L`, tillr builds the inverse of `L` **on the tip** and lands it when it applies cleanly, the fast gate passes on the tip with the revert, `K` passes on the tip with the revert, no landed item depends on `L`'s item, and `L` is not itself a remedy.

What the code does instead is narrower:

- In `HeavyRunner.offerRevert` ([`tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy.go)), **before** `r.Revert` (`Loop.revertLanding`) is called, `r.requireCurrentTip(ctx, tip)` compares `refs/heads/main` against `run.TipSHA` (`tip`). Whenever any candidate landed during the proof or `FindFirstRed` bisection, `offerRevert` immediately returns `RevertOutcome{Refused: "trunk advanced beyond the proved commit"}` (`revertFromGateReplay` in [`tools/tillr-drain/drain/heavy_revert.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/heavy_revert.go) has the parallel guard `"trunk advanced beyond the confirmed red commit"`).
- Inside `Loop.revertLanding` ([`tools/tillr-drain/drain/heavy_revert.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/heavy_revert.go)), commit `b1809482` added `confirmedRedTip` and `tipChecksPass` so a revert is dropped (`Result: "dropped"`) when a moved tip already passes `K` — yet `buildRevert(ctx, work, tip, landing, of, ids)` still checks out `tip` (`run.TipSHA`) rather than `confirmedRedTip` and sets `candidate.BaseSHA = tip`.
- After `RetainRedRepairs` runs, `judgeTip` ([`tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy.go)) appends `"automatic revert not possible: " + HeavyBrief(check.RevertRefused)` onto `run.Checks[i].Detail` whenever `check.FirstRedLanding != ""` and `check.RevertRefused != ""`, producing the fourth deferral's exact message.

### 1.4 `RetainRedRepairs` defers on unverified input touches, miswrites `trunk-repair.json`, and alternates past `priorDeferredChecks`

1. **Unverified input-touch deferral (`RetainRedRepairs` in [`tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy_attribution.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy_attribution.go)).** When `main` (`tip`) has moved past `run.TipSHA`:
   - For a focused test check with `len(check.Reads) == 0` (`default:` branch), `RetainRedRepairs` runs `r.Replay(ctx, tip, check.CheckID, ...)` and defers **only on a demonstrated pass** (`"the focused check passed on the advanced trunk"`).
   - For a declaration check with `len(check.Reads) != 0` (`case len(check.Reads) != 0:`), `RetainRedRepairs` **never replays or checks whether `tip` passes**: if any commit in `run.TipSHA..tip` touched any path matching `fixer.ReadsCoverPath(check.Reads, path)`, it sets `reason = "newer commits touched a declared input: " + path` and drops the check from `groups[sha]` so `r.Fix` files no `fixer-main-<sha>` for that proof.
2. **False repair marker in `.git/tillr-gate/trunk-repair.json`.** On every such deferral, `RetainRedRepairs` calls `fixer.RecordTrunkRepair(ctx, r.Root, tip, note)` ([`tools/tillr-drain/fixer/trunk_repair.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/fixer/trunk_repair.go)), overwriting `.git/tillr-gate/trunk-repair.json` with `tip` as if `tip` had *repaired* the trunk. `RecordedTrunkRepair` then reports `tip` to `preRebaseRepairedTrunk` ([`tools/tillr-drain/drain/trunk_repair.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/trunk_repair.go)), forcing ordinary candidates cut from older bases to rebase onto `main` before their fast gate starts — adding pre-gate rebase churn on top of a trunk that is still red.
3. **Why `priorDeferredChecks` (commit `31d4fc78`) still allows unbounded deferral cycles.** `priorDeferredChecks(dir, run.TipSHA)` in [`tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy_attribution.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy_attribution.go) reads **only** the single most recent `heavy.json` (`fixer.DeferredRepairPrefixes(check.Detail)`):
   - On proof *N*, `priorDeferred[check.CheckID]` is `false`, so `RetainRedRepairs` defers (`heavy.json` carries `"repair deferred to proof of ..."`).
   - On proof *N+1*, `priorDeferred[check.CheckID]` is `true`, so `RetainRedRepairs` does not defer and writes `heavy.json` **without** `"repair deferred to proof of ..."` (or an intervening `"unknown"` run causes `clearPriorUnknownHeavy` in [`tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy.go) to remove `heavy.json`).
   - On proof *N+2* — whenever any open `fixer-main-<sha>` is still running in its lane or held blocked — `priorDeferredChecks` reads proof *N+1*'s `heavy.json`, sees `false` again, and defers again (`defer -> non-defer -> defer -> non-defer -> defer`), advancing `trunk-repair.json` on every odd red proof without bound. Comparing only against the immediately preceding record is the exact two-step alternation defect [`D-GP11`](./tillr-graph-is-the-path.md#decision-ledger) ([Section 7](./tillr-graph-is-the-path.md#7-what-stops-repetition)) rejected.

## 2. Four options for repairing a red proof on a moving trunk

*Provenance: Options **A**, **B**, **C**, and **D** below are the designer's options prepared from an operator request; none is an owner ruling, and the owner chooses among them in [`OQ-TRM1`](#OQ-TRM1).*

| Option | How revert handles an advanced trunk | How `go-test-tillr-drain` is replayed / attributed | When repair may be deferred (and what `trunk-repair.json` records) | What happens to ordinary `GatePassed` landings while `go-test-tillr-drain` is red |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A. Revert on `currentTip` + focused `go-test:` case extraction + never defer on unverified input touches** *(designer's leaning)* | Build, gate, and verify the inverse of `L` on `confirmedRedTip` (`currentTip`), and squash onto `main` via `tillr-land` if disjoint landings arrive during the revert gate | Extract concrete failing Go test cases (`go-test:<module>:<pkg>:<TestName>`) from declaration logs (`ReadTriageBlock` / `ReadGoTestFailures`), keeping whole-check fallback for build/unnamed failures and [`D-GR31`](../reference/gate.md#D-GR31) skip for timeouts/deadlocks | **Only on a demonstrated pass** of the failing check / extracted Go test case(s) on `currentTip` (or trunk rewrite); `trunk-repair.json` written **only** on an actual revert or demonstrated pass | Disjoint and non-conflicting fast-gate candidates keep landing (`D-GR53` / `D-GR56`); revert lands in seconds/minutes on `currentTip`, or `fixer-main-<sha>` files immediately at `repair` priority ([`OQ-GR7`](../reference/gate.md#OQ-GR7)) |
| **B. Option A + enforce [`OQ-GP7`](./tillr-graph-is-the-path.md#decision-ledger) on `GatePassed` candidates against red heavy checks** | Same as Option A (revert on `confirmedRedTip` + `tillr-land` squash) | Same as Option A (`go-test:` case extraction with whole-check fallback) | Same as Option A (**only** on a demonstrated pass; `trunk-repair.json` only on proved repair) | Before landing a `GatePassed` candidate that touches a red heavy check's `reads` (`22` entries on `go-test-tillr-drain`), either run the failing `go-test:` case(s) / heavy check at its gate or hold it as `trunk-already-red` behind the remedy |
| **C. Revert on `currentTip` + never defer on unverified input touches (keep monolithic `go-test-tillr-drain` declaration replays)** | Same as Option A (revert on `confirmedRedTip` + `tillr-land` squash) | Unchanged from `HEAD`: every bisection, `tipChecksPass`, and inverse check runs the whole `go-test-tillr-drain` declaration (`6` shards) | **Never** for `len(check.Reads) != 0` without a demonstrated pass (deletes input-touch deferral and `priorDeferredChecks`); `trunk-repair.json` only on proved repair | Same as Option A (`GatePassed` candidates keep landing; `fixer-main-<sha>` files immediately when revert cannot land) |
| **D. Episode-keyed single deferral (`<= 1` per `(green_sha, check_id)`) + revert on `currentTip` afterward** | Same as Option A once the single deferral is spent | Unchanged from `HEAD` (or paired with case extraction) | Allows **at most one** input-touch deferral per `(BaseSHA, CheckID)` episode in `heavy-attributions.json` (never writing `trunk-repair.json` on deferral), then files `fixer-main-<sha>` and never defers again until `refs/tillr/green` moves or the check passes | Same as Option A, except the **first** red proof on a busy trunk still waits one extra full proof (`~15`–`25 min`) with no revert and no open `fixer-main-<sha>` |

### 2.1 Option A — Revert on `currentTip`, extract failing Go test cases for focused replay, and never defer on an unverified input touch *(designer's leaning)*

1. **Build and verify automatic reverts on `currentTip` ([`OQ-GP1`](./tillr-graph-is-the-path.md#decision-ledger), [`D-GR6`](../reference/gate.md#D-GR6), [Landing Principles §2](./landing-principles.md#2-re-application-preserves-the-change-not-the-patch) & [§3](./landing-principles.md#3-gate-what-will-be-the-tip-once)).**
   - Remove `r.requireCurrentTip(ctx, tip)` from `HeavyRunner.offerRevert` ([`tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy.go)) and remove the `currentTip != tip` refusal from `Loop.revertFromGateReplay` ([`tools/tillr-drain/drain/heavy_revert.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/heavy_revert.go)).
   - In `Loop.revertLanding` ([`tools/tillr-drain/drain/heavy_revert.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/heavy_revert.go)), read `cur` (`refs/heads/main`): when `cur != tip`, check `tipChecksPass(ctx, cur, ids, replay)` as `b1809482` already does (dropping the revert with `Result: "dropped"` and recording `fixer.RecordTrunkRepair` if `cur` already passes), and when `cur` is still red, call `buildRevert(ctx, work, confirmedRedTip, landing, of, ids)` with `candidate.BaseSHA = confirmedRedTip` rather than `run.TipSHA`.
   - Gate the revert candidate once (`Steps.Gate` + `replay(ctx, revert, id)` for each attributed check `id`). If `main` advances again while the revert's fast gate runs, let `watchWG` drop the revert only if the new tip already passes `ids`; otherwise hand `candidate` (`BaseSHA: confirmedRedTip`, `HeadSHA: revert`) directly to `landWithActions` (`tools/tillr-land`), where `Trunk.Squash` ([`tools/tillr-land/land/trunk.go`](../../tools/tillr-land/land/trunk.go)) three-way merges the verified inverse onto the new trunk tip without re-gating — refusing only on an actual `squash-conflict` (in which case `revertLanding` returns `"not-possible"` and `fixer-main-<L>` is filed).
2. **Extract named Go test failures from declaration Go suites (`go-test-tillr-*`) for fast bisection, moved-tip checks, and inverse verification.**
   - When a `go-test-tillr-*` declaration check fails in `JudgeHeavy` ([`tools/tillr-drain/internal/gatespans/heavy_steps.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/internal/gatespans/heavy_steps.go)), read its kept `.triage.log` / `.log` via `ReadTriageBlock` and `ReadGoTestFailures` ([`tools/tillr-drain/internal/runhistory/refusal_cases.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/internal/runhistory/refusal_cases.go)) and emit per-case `go-test:<module>:<pkg>:<TestName>` checks (reusing `GoSuiteFailures` and `JudgeHeavyGo` in [`tools/tillr-drain/internal/gatespans/heavy_go.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/internal/gatespans/heavy_go.go)).
   - Preserve [`D-GR31`](../reference/gate.md#D-GR31): any case whose output contains Go's test-timeout panic or runtime deadlock sets `GoTimedOut = true` and skips solo replay, filing one fixer against the tip. Any build failure, crash, or log without named cases retains the parent `go-test-tillr-*` check as today.
   - Effect: `FindFirstRed` bisection, `tipChecksPass` on an advanced trunk, `OneLogged` inverse verification, and `RetainRedRepairs` each run `go test -count=1 -run ^TestName$ ./pkg` in **seconds** instead of running `6` whole-module shards (`~8`–`20 min`) per commit, and a reverted item's `requiredChecks` (`heavy_revert.go`) runs the exact `go-test:` selector or parent check before re-landing.
3. **Eliminate unverified input-touch deferral and false `trunk-repair.json` writes ([Landing Principle §11](./landing-principles.md#11-a-failure-is-diagnosed-not-waited-out)).**
   - In `RetainRedRepairs` ([`tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy_attribution.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy_attribution.go)), delete `case len(check.Reads) != 0:`'s `"newer commits touched a declared input"` branch and delete `priorDeferredChecks`.
   - Defer filing a fixer **only** when (a) `!ancestor` (`"trunk was rewritten"`) or (b) a focused check (`len(check.Reads) == 0`, including extracted `go-test:` cases) is **demonstrated to pass** on `tip` (`"the focused check passed on the advanced trunk"`). For a monolithic check that was not replayed on `tip` (such as a timeout or unnamed build failure), never defer: file or attach `fixer-main-<sha>` immediately (`linkMatchingOpenFixers` in [`tools/tillr-drain/drain/heavy_loop.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/heavy_loop.go) deduplicates to one open fixer per failing check episode under [`D-FG2`](../reference/gate.md#D-FG2)).
   - Call `fixer.RecordTrunkRepair` **only** when a revert lands (`check.RevertedBy != ""`) or a previously failing check is demonstrated to pass on `tip` — never on an unproved deferral — so `preRebaseRepairedTrunk` ([`tools/tillr-drain/drain/trunk_repair.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/trunk_repair.go)) never rebases ordinary candidates onto an unrepaired trunk.
   - Note on sliceability ([Landing Principle §15](./landing-principles.md#15-land-the-smallest-functional-change)): Part 1 + Part 3 (Option C below) and Part 2 (`go-test:` extraction for declaration suites) are two small, independently functional commits.

### 2.2 Option B — Option A plus enforcing [`OQ-GP7`](./tillr-graph-is-the-path.md#decision-ledger) on `GatePassed` candidates against red heavy checks

- **Mechanism:** Build all three parts of Option A, **and** extend `Loop`'s post-gate settlement ([`tools/tillr-drain/drain/loop.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/loop.go), [`tools/tillr-drain/drain/trunk.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/trunk.go)) so that when a completed proof (`heavy.json`) or an open `fixer-main-*` records a heavy check `K` as red on the trunk, a non-remedy candidate whose fast gate answered `GatePassed` is still checked against `K`:
  - **Sub-variant B1 (declaration `reads`):** compare the candidate's changed paths against `K`'s declared `reads` (`22` entries for `go-test-tillr-drain.json`). ### Why Option A (landed as Option C first, then `go-test:` extraction) beats Options B, C-alone, and D *(designer's analysis)*

1. **Why unverified input-touch deferral (current `HEAD` and Option D) is pointed the wrong way.** `RetainRedRepairs`'s `len(check.Reads) != 0` branch assumed a declaration check's `reads` is narrow enough that a commit touching `reads` probably fixed or changed the failure. For `go-test-tillr-drain.json`, `reads` covers `22` paths across all `11` `tools/tillr*` Go modules (`D-GR54`). An edit to `tools/tillr-run/native/fence.go` has almost zero chance of fixing a failing `drain` test, yet `100%` chance of matching `check.Reads`. Waiting another proof without a demonstrated pass fails [Landing Principle §11](./landing-principles.md#11-a-failure-is-diagnosed-not-waited-out)'s single question (*"what will be different on the next run, and how will the system know?"*). And if a concurrent landing *did* fix the failure while `fixer-main-<sha>` was queued or running, nothing is wasted: under Option A, `tipChecksPass` drops the revert before landing (`Result: "dropped"`), and for an open `fixer-main-<sha>`, the next green proof or focused check proof resolves the fixer (`D-JL36` in [`tillr-judge-and-lifecycle.md`](./tillr-judge-and-lifecycle.md#423-a-later-green-proof-resolves-a-trunk-fixer-whose-worker-has-finished-and-whose-candidate-waits-to-gate-d-jl36), `fixer_resolve.go`).
2. **Why `offerRevert` must build on `currentTip` and squash via `tillr-land`.** Proofs take minutes (`8`–`20+ min`); landings take seconds. Refusing `offerRevert` whenever `main != run.TipSHA` disables automatic revert ([`OQ-GP1`](./tillr-graph-is-the-path.md#decision-ledger) / [`D-GR6`](../reference/gate.md#D-GR6)) at the exact throughput levels [`D-GR2`](../reference/gate.md#D-GR2) requires. Building the inverse of `L` on `confirmedRedTip`, gating it once, and letting `Trunk.Squash` three-way merge it onto `main` applies [Landing Principles §2](./landing-principles.md#2-re-application-preserves-the-change-not-the-patch) and [§3](./landing-principles.md#3-gate-what-will-be-the-tip-once) to reverts the same way ordinary candidates use them.
3. **Why Option A beats Option C alone, while Option C is the cleanest Slice 1.** Without `go-test:` case extraction (Option C), every `FindFirstRed` step, every moved-tip `tipChecksPass` check, and every inverse check with `L` reverted re-runs `go-test-tillr-drain` (`6` shards) rather than the single failing `TestName` (`~1`–`5 s`). Extracting `go-test:<module>:<pkg>:<TestName>` from declaration logs (already built for legacy suites in `GoSuiteFailures` / `JudgeHeavyGo` and already parsed in `ReadTriageBlock` / `ReadGoTestFailures`) cuts both bisection and `revertLanding` verification from minutes to seconds so `main` rarely moves even once while the revert gates. Landing Option C as Slice 1 and declaration `go-test:` extraction as Slice 2 obeys [Landing Principle §15](./landing-principles.md#15-land-the-smallest-functional-change).
4. **Why Option A leaves `GatePassed` unheld rather than Option B, unless the owner wants strict heavy-check freezes.** Under [`OQ-GP7`](./tillr-graph-is-the-path.md#decision-ledger), holding intersecting candidates works cleanly when a check's `reads` matches the narrow code it tests. Because [`D-GR54`](../reference/gate.md#D-GR54) / [`D-GR56`](../reference/gate.md#D-GR56) gave `go-test-tillr-drain.json` `22` repository-wide `reads` entries, enforcing [`OQ-GP7`](./tillr-graph-is-the-path.md#decision-ledger) on `GatePassed` candidates against `go-test-tillr-drain` (Option B) turns any single failing `drain` test into a near-total freeze across all `11` `tools/tillr*` modules. Once Option A makes `currentTip` automatic revert land in seconds (and files `repair`-priority `fixer-main-<sha>` immediately on overlap, with `FindFirstRed` reusing prior episode attributions in `heavy-attributions.json`), `refs/tillr/green` ([`D-GR5`](../reference/gate.md#D-GR5)) recovers promptly without freezing `11` modules of unrelated work. That trade-off between [`OQ-GP7`](./tillr-graph-is-the-path.md#decision-ledger) and [`D-GR53`](../reference/gate.md#D-GR53) / [`D-GR56`](../reference/gate.md#D-GR56) is a policy choice only the owner can make, so [`OQ-TRM1`](#OQ-TRM1) puts Option A vs. Option B (alongside C and D) directly to the owner.
