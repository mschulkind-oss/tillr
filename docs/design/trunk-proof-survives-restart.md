# A loop restart never waits on the trunk proof, because the proof survives the restart

**Status:** Open for the owner's decision in [`OQ-TPS1`](#OQ-TPS1); **this document settles nothing itself**. Every option, cost comparison, ruling check, and leaning below is recorded from an operator request as **the designer's analysis and leaning, not an owner ruling** ([decision provenance review](../tillr/decision-provenance-review.md#6-how-this-stops-happening), [`OQ-PR21`](../tillr/decision-provenance-review.md#decision-ledger), [`OQ-PR22`](../tillr/decision-provenance-review.md#decision-ledger), `D-PR1`, `D-PR2`). Nothing is changed in Go code or configuration until the owner rules on [`OQ-TPS1`](#OQ-TPS1).

> **In short.** Today a landing gate (`--tillr-gate-supervisor`) runs in its own session watching `(OwnerPID, OwnerStartTicks)` and survives `tillr-drain`'s in-place `syscall.Exec`, whereas the after-merge trunk proof (`HeavyRunner.RunAttempt`) runs inside the `tillr-drain` process holding a close-on-exec death pipe (`HeavyOwnership`) to `--tillr-heavy-supervisor`. Because `syscall.Exec` closes that pipe and kills the proof, `gateRestartWith` was made to wait on `restartProofActive()` (`f43f6cc4`). On **2026-11-25** (through `16:56:36 EST`, `seq = 43970`), `tillr-drain` spent **`682.69 min` (`11 h 22.69 min`)** across `21` pending-restart windows waiting to replace itself, including **`65.65 min`** holding new gate starts (`W12`–`W13`) and **`126.77 min`** holding **both** new gate starts and worker dispatches (`W14`–`W21`, `114.66 min` after post-landing `roll`/`push` actions finished). Three consecutive afternoon stalls (`17m 17.5s`, `18m 33.9s`, and `22m 52.5s`) froze gate starts for `16.2`–`20.9 min` and worker dispatches for `16.4`–`17.6 min` until the operator ran `just tillr-restart` by hand — and across the day **every hand restart or manual process replacement during an active proof (`5` of `5`: `4` `drain.stop-requested` restarts plus `1` manual process replacement, `129.66 min` of proof work)** cancelled the running trunk proof or its `SearchFirstRed` culprit search, culminating in Episode `#23` (`26.92 min` across `18` unproved commits / `13` touching check `Reads`, killed `35 s` into replaying an `8m 17.2s` `go-test-tillr-drain` failure). Moving the proof and its culprit search into a detached supervisor re-attached across `syscall.Exec` removes `restartProofActive()` completely so in-place restarts never wait on the proof and never hold gate starts or worker dispatches behind one.

## 2. Why landing gates survive `syscall.Exec` today and the trunk proof does not

`tillr-drain` already runs two kinds of child supervisors, and their process-lifetime contracts are opposite:

1. **Detached landing gates (`--tillr-gate-supervisor`) survive `syscall.Exec`.** In `ProcessSteps.Gate` and `MaybeRunGateSupervisor` ([`tools/tillr-drain/drain/steps.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/steps.go)) together with `releaseRestartExaminations` and `reclaimDetachedGateExaminationsWith` ([`tools/tillr-drain/drain/restart_examination.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/restart_examination.go)):
   - `tillr-drain` spawns `--tillr-gate-supervisor` in its own session (`Setsid: true`), closes all parent pipes immediately, and writes `gate.face.json` containing `(OwnerPID, OwnerStartTicks)` (`processStartTicks(os.Getpid())`, field `22` of `/proc/<pid>/stat`).
   - Every `50 ms`, `--tillr-gate-supervisor` checks `ownerAlive(OwnerPID, OwnerStartTicks)` (`processStartTicks(pid) == ticks`).
   - Because `syscall.Exec` replaces the process image in place **without changing `PID` or `OwnerStartTicks`** ([`tillr-restart.md` Invariants](../reference/tillr-restart.md#invariants-and-warnings)), `gateRestartWith` does not wait for detached gates: `releaseRestartExaminations` leaves `gate.face.json` untouched, `syscall.Exec` runs immediately, and the new binary's `reclaimDetachedGateExaminationsWith` re-attaches a watcher until `gate.done.json` appears. If `tillr-drain` crashes or stops (`PID` exits), `ownerAlive` turns `false` within `50 ms` and `--tillr-gate-supervisor` sends `SIGKILL` to `-pgid`.
2. **The trunk proof (`HeavyRunner.RunAttempt`) dies on `syscall.Exec` for three mechanical reasons:**
   - **A parent-held close-on-exec death pipe (`HeavyOwnership`).** `StartHeavyOwnership` and `MaybeRunHeavySupervisor` ([`tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy_supervisor.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy_supervisor.go)) pass a close-on-exec pipe (`fd 3` ack, `fd 4` owner) from `tillr-drain` to `--tillr-heavy-supervisor`. The instant `tillr-drain` calls `syscall.Exec`, the kernel closes `HeavyOwnership.owner`; `--tillr-heavy-supervisor` reads `EOF` on `fd 4`, writes `heavy.cleanup-pending` (or `heavy.replay-cleanup-pending`), and sends `SIGKILL` to `-pgid` — violating [the second restart invariant](../reference/tillr-restart.md#invariants-and-warnings) (*"No parent-held pipe to a child that must survive"*).
   - **Three close-on-exec `flock` descriptors held inside `tillr-drain`.** During `RunAttempt`, the `tillr-drain` process holds `<git-common-dir>/tillr-loop.lock` (`AcquireLoopLock` in [`tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy_authority.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy_authority.go)), `.tillr/lanes/.rolling/heavy.lock` (`acquireHeavyLock`), and `<git-common-dir>/tillr-gate/heavy-running.lock` (`writeRunningStatus` in [`tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy_progress.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy_progress.go)). All three close on `syscall.Exec`.
   - **In-process multi-step coordination (`judgeTip` -> `heavyEarly` -> `SearchFirstRed` -> settlement).** `HeavyRunner.RunAttempt` ([`tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy.go)) and `heavyEarly` ([`tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy_early.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/heavy_early.go)) hold check outcomes, concurrent early bisection in `.rolling/worktree-replay`, `SearchFirstRed` bisection state, and `offerRevert` / `Fix` / `refs/tillr/green` callbacks in Go memory inside `tillr-drain`.
3. **Why `f43f6cc4`..`be505adc` could not escape the dilemma.** Because `syscall.Exec` killed `HeavyRunner.RunAttempt`, `f43f6cc4` added `restartProofActive()` to `gateRestartWith` ([`tools/tillr-drain/drain/restart.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/restart.go)). Once `syscall.Exec` waited `15`–`60 min` on the proof, every policy for what to do during that wait broke a standing rule: letting work start ran the pre-roll binary for up to an hour (`W01`–`W13`, `D-RTP1`), yielding mid-bisection (`5df4b4c0`) threw away culprit attribution (`Episodes #08`–`#10`), and holding new work (`f0eb8fa0`, `555c0414`, `0afa06bb`, `be505adc`) froze all lanes (`W14`–`W21`) and provoked hand restarts (`just tillr-restart`) that stopped `OwnerPID` and killed the proof anyway.

### 3.1 Option A — Detached trunk proof and culprit search re-attached across `syscall.Exec` like a landing gate *(designer's leaning)*

- **Mechanism:** Apply the exact `--tillr-gate-supervisor` process-supervision contract ([`tools/tillr-drain/drain/steps.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/steps.go), [`tools/tillr-drain/drain/restart_examination.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/restart_examination.go)) to the trunk proof and its `SearchFirstRed` culprit search:
  1. **Detached `--tillr-heavy-proof` supervisor in `heavyrun` ([`D-GR47`](../reference/gate.md#D-GR47) preserved).** Instead of running `HeavyRunner.RunAttempt` inside a `tillr-drain` goroutine over a parent-held `HeavyOwnership` pipe, `startHeavy` verifies `proofCommonDir` and spawns `tillr-drain --tillr-heavy-proof` in its own session (`Setsid: true`, `ProcessClassProof` priority `nice` / cgroup), closing all parent descriptors immediately. `--tillr-heavy-proof` itself acquires `<git-common-dir>/tillr-loop.lock`, `.tillr/lanes/.rolling/heavy.lock`, and `<git-common-dir>/tillr-gate/heavy-running.lock` (`heavy-running.json`), writes `<git-common-dir>/tillr-gate/heavy-attempt.face.json` (`tip_sha`, `base_sha`, `pid`, `start_ticks`, `owner_pid`, `owner_start_ticks`, `started_at_ms`), and watches `ownerAlive(OwnerPID, OwnerStartTicks)` every `50 ms`.
  2. **Both `judgeTip` and `SearchFirstRed` run inside `--tillr-heavy-proof`.** `--tillr-heavy-proof` runs `tillr-gate checks --heavy-tree` (with stall retry, `heavy-progress.json`, and `drain.heavy-progress` events) **and** runs `heavyEarly` (`.rolling/worktree-replay`) plus post-tip `SearchFirstRed` bisection across `base_sha..tip_sha` (`heavy-replays.json`, `heavy-attributions.json`), then atomically writes `<git-common-dir>/tillr-gate/heavy-attempt.done.json` (`HeavyRun` check list, durations, `FirstRedLanding` attributions, `GateFault`, log refs) before releasing its locks and exiting.
  3. **Authoritative settlement stays in `tillr-drain` (`Loop`).** Just as `--tillr-gate-supervisor` writes `gate.done.json` while `Loop` performs `tillr-land` / examination settlement, whichever `tillr-drain` image is running when `heavy-attempt.done.json` appears (either the original loop or the replacement binary via `reclaimDetachedHeavyProof` after `syscall.Exec`) settles `refs/tillr/green` (`update-ref` CAS), `PublishHeavy` (`heavy.json` + `green-<tip>.json`), `l.revertLanding` (`auto_revert`), `l.fileTrunkFixerNoted` (`fixer-main-<sha>`), and `green` push. For concurrent `heavyEarly` reverts (when a fast heavy check like `go-affected-test` finishes `SearchFirstRed` while `go-test-tillr-drain` is still running), `--tillr-heavy-proof` writes `heavy-early.request.json` and the current `Loop` executes `l.revertLanding` and writes `heavy-early.answer.json` — surviving `syscall.Exec` mid-proof without importing `drain` into `heavyrun` ([`D-GR47`](../reference/gate.md#D-GR47)).
  4. **Delete `restartProofActive()` and `allowDuringProof`.** `gateRestartWith` (`drain/restart.go`) waits only on `restartActionDeferred()` (an active `rollAction` / `pushAction`) and `restartMechanical.TryLock()`. `releaseRestartExaminations` leaves both `gate.face.json` and `heavy-attempt.face.json` running across `syscall.Exec`, and `heavy.ReadStatus` (`tillr-view` and `tillr drain why`) sees `heavy-running.lock` held without interruption across the exec (`3c5d1dc1`).
- **Engineering and runtime cost:** ~`350`–`500` lines of Go across `tools/tillr-drain/heavyrun/` and `tools/tillr-drain/drain/` (offset by deleting `restartProofActive`, `allowDuringProof`, `lockRestartLanding`, and their proof-wait workarounds) plus Linux integration tests proving `syscall.Exec` re-attachment during both `judgeTip` and `SearchFirstRed` and `50 ms` reaping on owner death (`TestHeavyReapLinux`). Runtime overhead is one lightweight supervisor process and `50 ms` `/proc/<pid>/stat` polling (`~0%` CPU, `< 15 MB` RSS, identical to `--tillr-gate-supervisor`).
- **What work it can lose:**
  - **In-place restart (`tillr-drain restart` / `just tillr-roll` -> `syscall.Exec`):** **Zero work lost (`0` checks, `0` `SearchFirstRed` replays, `0` early reverts).**
  - **Hand restart (`just tillr-restart` -> `tillr-drain stop` + rebuild + new `PID`):** Because `tillr-drain stop` exits `OwnerPID`, `--tillr-heavy-proof` reaps `-pgid` within `50 ms` just like `--tillr-gate-supervisor`, losing **at most the single check or `SearchFirstRed` replay currently executing** (up to `~8`–`10 min` if `go-test-tillr-drain` is mid-flight; all prior checks in `heavy-progress.json` and prior replays in `heavy-replays.json` are already saved on disk). Crucially, on 2026-11-25 all three afternoon hand restarts (`W19`–`W21`) happened *only* because `tillr-drain restart` wedged behind `restartProofActive()`; once `syscall.Exec` answers in `< 2 s`, code rolls never force a hand restart.
- **Are gate starts or worker dispatches ever held?** **Never behind a trunk proof or culprit search (`0 ms`).** Gate starts and worker dispatches pause only for the sub-second `restartMechanical` drain window (`< 100 ms`–`2 s` while an already-running `tillr-land` squash or `cmd.Start` releases its read lock so `gateRestartWith` can `syscall.Exec` without `TryLock` starvation, `15494d44` / [`D-SN2`](./tillr-scheduling-and-notifications.md#D-SN2)).

### 3.2 Option B — Detached trunk proof and culprit search surviving both `syscall.Exec` and `just tillr-restart` (`stop` without `--end-runs`)

- **Mechanism:** Build Option A's detached `--tillr-heavy-proof` supervisor, `heavy-attempt.{face,done}.json` protocol, and `restartProofActive()` removal, **plus** decouple `--tillr-heavy-proof` from `OwnerPID` exit on graceful `tillr-drain stop` (`--end-runs=false`, which `just tillr-restart` runs) so its lifetime matches `tillr-run exec` worker faces ([`tillr-restart.md`](../reference/tillr-restart.md#tillr-drain-keep-just-tillr-keep-and-just-tillr-restart)):
  - On `syscall.Exec`, `--tillr-heavy-proof` continues untouched and is re-attached by the new binary.
  - On `tillr-drain stop` without `--end-runs` (including `just tillr-restart`), `stop` records a detached state in `heavy-attempt.face.json` instead of letting `--tillr-heavy-proof` kill `-pgid`; `--tillr-heavy-proof` finishes its running check and `SearchFirstRed` replays headless while the loop is down (`16.5`–`18.6 s` during `just tillr-restart` on 2026-11-25), and the newly started `tillr-drain loop` (`new PID`) re-adopts `heavy-attempt.face.json` and updates `(OwnerPID, OwnerStartTicks)`.
  - On `tillr-drain stop --end-runs`, `stop` explicitly signals `--tillr-heavy-proof`'s process group alongside worker sessions.
- **Engineering and runtime cost:** ~`500`–`700` lines of Go (Option A plus headless PID-transfer state, `stop --end-runs` signaling for `--tillr-heavy-proof`, and handling `heavyEarly` requests when no loop is running by queueing them until a loop re-attaches). Steady-state CPU/RAM while a loop runs is zero, **but** stopping or `SIGKILL`-crashing `tillr-drain` without `--end-runs` leaves a `16`-shard `go-test-tillr-drain` suite running headless in the background for up to `20`–`60 min` unless an adoption-timeout lease is added.
- **What work it can lose:** **Zero work lost on `syscall.Exec` (`0` checks, `0` replays) and zero work lost on `just tillr-restart` (`0` checks, `0` replays).** Work is cancelled only when the operator explicitly passes `tillr-drain stop --end-runs` (losing the single in-flight check/replay).
- **Are gate starts or worker dispatches ever held?** **Never behind a trunk proof or culprit search (`0 ms`)** — only the same sub-second `restartMechanical` drain window (`< 100 ms`–`2 s`) immediately before `syscall.Exec`.

### 3.3 Option C — Keep the proof in-process and yield `syscall.Exec` between individual checks and `SearchFirstRed` replays

- **Mechanism:** Keep `HeavyRunner.RunAttempt` in-process inside `tillr-drain`, split `tillr-gate checks --heavy-tree` into per-check invocations persisted in `heavy-progress.json`, restore `BeforeReplay` (fixing `5df4b4c0`'s bug by persisting the failing `tip_sha` so the post-`exec` binary resumes `SearchFirstRed` on that **same** `tip_sha` before advancing to newer commits), and on `drain.restart-request` either **(C1)** wait for the single running check/replay to finish before `syscall.Exec` or **(C2)** `syscall.Exec` immediately — letting `HeavyOwnership` EOF cancel either the single running check/replay (checkpointed variant) or the whole proof/search (`5df4b4c0` / pre-`f43f6cc4` baseline).
- **Engineering and runtime cost:** ~`200`–`350` lines of Go (`per-check` heavy runner + durable `pending-attribution.json` pinned to `tip_sha`). Runtime cost is steep in either sub-variant: `go-test-tillr-drain` alone runs for **`8.29`–`20.89 min`** (`497 s` in `#23`, `622 s` in `#21`, `1,048 s` in `#08`, `1,253 s` in `#01`), so **C1** still blocks restarts for up to **`21 min`**, while **C2** throws away and re-runs **`8`–`21 min`** of `16`-shard Go test CPU on every `tools/` landing that rolls while `go-test-tillr-drain` is executing.
- **What work it can lose:**
  - **C1 (wait for check/replay boundary):** `0` work lost on `syscall.Exec` if operators wait up to `21 min`; loses `1` check/replay (`<= 21 min`) on `just tillr-restart`.
  - **C2 (cancel in-flight check/replay on `syscall.Exec`):** loses up to **`8`–`21 min`** of `go-test-tillr-drain` work on **every** `syscall.Exec` roll and on **every** `just tillr-restart` — under steady `tools/` landings (`6` landings in `50 s` in `W20`, `4` landings in `7 min` in `W21`), repeated rolls can prevent `go-test-tillr-drain` from ever finishing (`refs/tillr/green` livelock).
- **Are gate starts or worker dispatches ever held?**
  - **C1:** **Yes — held up to `8`–`21 min`** during `go-test-tillr-drain` (or if unheld, runs the stale pre-roll binary for `8`–`21 min`).
  - **C2:** Only the sub-second `restartMechanical` window (`< 2 s`), paid for by killing `8`–`21 min` test runs on every roll.

### 3.4 Option D — Keep the proof in-process and defer `syscall.Exec` across the full proof and culprit search (`D1`: current `HEAD`; `D2`: `D-RTP1`)

- **Mechanism:** Keep `HeavyRunner.RunAttempt` in-process and keep `restartProofActive()` in `gateRestartWith` (`f43f6cc4`, `d7595176`) so `syscall.Exec` waits `15`–`60 min` for the entire proof and `SearchFirstRed` culprit search to finish, while either:
  - **`D1` (current `HEAD` at `9a03769753ec` via `555c0414`, `0afa06bb`, `be505adc`):** hold all new fast gates, worker dispatches, examination claims, and conflict repairs until the restart answers; or
  - **`D2` (unbuilt designer decision [`D-RTP1`](./restart-during-trunk-proof.md#D-RTP1) of 2026-11-25):** exempt `restartProofActive()` from `restartGateStartHeld` / `buildRestartPending` so the old pre-roll `tillr-drain` binary keeps starting detached gates and workers for the entire `15`–`60 min` proof, holding only during the final `< 2 s` `restartMechanical` drain once the proof ends.
- **Engineering and runtime cost:** `0` LOC for `D1` (already on `main`); ~`80`–`150` LOC for `D2`. Runtime cost in `D1` is **`16`–`35 min` multi-lane stalls** on every `tools/` roll during a proof (`Section 1.2`); runtime cost in `D2` is running **stale, already-superseded `tillr-drain` scheduling, staging, conflict-fix, and gate-launch code for `15`–`60 min`** after `just tillr-roll` asked to replace it (the exact failure mode `555c0414` was filed to stop after build `e59c6e33` dispatched `-a2-n1` runs under old logic).
- **What work it can lose:** `0` on `syscall.Exec` *only if* no operator runs `just tillr-restart` during the `15`–`60 min` wait; on `just tillr-restart` (which `D1` forced `3` times in one afternoon on 2026-11-25 and `D2` still forces whenever an operator needs a `tillr-drain` bug fix active immediately), kills the entire active check or `SearchFirstRed` replay (`20.1`–`30.5 min` episodes lost on 2026-11-25).

  - **`D1`:** **Yes — both gate starts and worker dispatches are held `11`–`35 min` per proof** (`162.98 min` gate holds and `114.66 min` dispatch holds measured on 2026-11-25).
