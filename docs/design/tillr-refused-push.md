# When the remote has moved, the drain rebases unpublished commits onto it and proves the new tip instead of retrying a push that cannot fast-forward

**Status:** 2026-11-20 — decided; partially built at `2323ec315`. Post-hoc `git pull --rebase` absorption via `trunk_rewrites` landed in `98cc40ffe`, minimal refused-push detection and clean linear replay in `tillr-drain` landed in `8fe514714` (kept as built for clean replays), and upstream-identity trust via `refs/tillr/published/` landed in `97d37524b` ([OQ-RP3](#OQ-RP3)). Conflict repair with escalation ([OQ-RP2](#OQ-RP2), `D-RP5`), full `refs/tillr/published/` bookkeeping and force-push detection (`D-RP2`), distinct failure classes for server-side declines and held patch moves (`D-RP1`), direct `trunk_rewrites` recording when diffs change (`D-RP3`), and waiting/status/`tillr drain push` surfaces (`D-RP6`) remain unbuilt and queued. [OQ-RP1](#OQ-RP1), [OQ-RP2](#OQ-RP2), and [OQ-RP3](#OQ-RP3) were ruled by the owner on 2026-11-20; six engineering choices (`D-RP1` through `D-RP6`) remain marked as the designer's decisions in the [Decision Ledger](#decision-ledger).

> **In short.** When an automatic push fails, the drain checks `git push --porcelain` and fetches the target ref to separate a moved remote from server-side declines, held patch moves, broker denials, and transport failures; replays unpublished local commits linearly onto forward remote commits in `tillr-drain` under `land.lock`, moves the main checkout via `git reset --keep`, and remaps recorded landing SHAs; trusts commits reachable from `refs/tillr/published/`; repairs textual conflicts automatically behind gate, review, and heavy proof with ordinary obstacle escalation; and reports every non-idle state with an honest mover and unpublished commit count.

**Why it matters.** Without automatic remote rebase and conflict repair, a commit pushed from another machine or merged on GitHub strands automatic pushes behind non-fast-forward rejections or manual rebase stops while operator surfaces mislabel push failures as waiting on the next green advance.
**The shape.** A two-step push classifier ([Section 3](#3-telling-a-refused-push-apart-from-a-failed-one)), linear rebase onto the fetched remote under `land.lock` in `tillr-drain` ([Section 4](#4-rebasing-unpublished-commits-onto-the-moved-remote)), proof before publishing conflict repairs while keeping `8fe514714`'s clean-replay flow ([Section 5](#5-proving-the-rebased-trunk-before-pushing)), automatic fenced conflict repair with standard escalations ([Section 6](#6-when-rebasing-onto-the-remote-conflicts)), and truthful operator surfaces ([Section 7](#7-showing-the-state-and-its-mover-while-it-lasts)).
**Cost.** Per-clone `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` bookkeeping and `boundary.go` excuse, `healRefusedPush` in `tillr-drain` under `land.lock` (`git reset --keep` plus `observeTrunk` patch matching on clean replays and direct `trunk_rewrites` recording when conflict repair or context shifts alter a commit's diff), an upstream-pointer exemption in `CheckIdentities` (built in `97d37524b`), one fenced repair run per textual rebase conflict, and a push-state projection in `tillr-view` and the Observatory header — keeping `main` linear with zero merge commits.
**Start at** [Section 1](#1-what-happens-today-and-which-principles-it-breaks) for today's gap, [Section 3](#3-telling-a-refused-push-apart-from-a-failed-one) and [Section 4](#4-rebasing-unpublished-commits-onto-the-moved-remote) for the core mechanism, or the [Decision Ledger](#decision-ledger) for the owner's three rulings and the six designer decisions.
**Needs your ruling:** None — [OQ-RP1](#OQ-RP1), [OQ-RP2](#OQ-RP2), and [OQ-RP3](#OQ-RP3) were ruled by the owner on 2026-11-20; six engineering choices (`D-RP1` through `D-RP6`) in the [Decision Ledger](#decision-ledger) remain marked as the designer's decisions under [OQ-PR21](../tillr/decision-provenance-review.md#OQ-PR21) and may be overturned by the owner.
**Reads with:** [Landing principles](./landing-principles.md), [Restarting tillr in place](../reference/tillr-restart.md), [The gate rework](./gate-rework.md), [The item graph is the whole path](./tillr-graph-is-the-path.md), and [One vocabulary for tillr](./tillr-vocabulary.md).

## 1. What happens today, and which principles it breaks

On 2026-11-19 the owner asked: *"what happens if remote moves before an autopush. auto rebase? something fixes conflicts automatically?"* Asked whether to file the gap, he said yes.

### 1.1 Today's push path

Automatic push is configured by `[push]` in [`.tillr.toml`](../../.tillr.toml), where `what` is `"none"`, `"green"` (this repository's setting, settled in [the restart reference](../reference/tillr-restart.md#pushing-what-the-proof-has-passed) as `D-RT10`), or `"tip"`, and `remote` and `ref` default to `refs/heads/main`'s upstream (`pushDestination` in [`post_landing.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/post_landing.go)).

1. **Trigger and single-slot queue.** `pushAction` runs in one background goroutine with a replaceable pending slot and a five-minute `pushTimeout`. In `"tip"` mode (`afterLandingCandidate`), each trunk landing submits `pushTrunk`, running `LC_ALL=C git push --porcelain -- <remote> refs/heads/main:<ref>`. In `"green"` mode (`afterGreen`), advancing `refs/tillr/green` after a passing heavy run — and once at loop startup — submits `pushGreen`, which skips when `refs/remotes/<remote>/<branch>` already contains `<green>` and otherwise pushes `<green>:<ref>` with no `+` or `--force*` flag. Every `git push` runs [`hooks/pre-push`](../../hooks/pre-push), which calls [`bin/patch-branches-publish`](../../bin/patch-branches-publish) to publish required `patch/<name>` branches.
2. **Minimal clean-replay heal (`8fe514714` and `97d37524b`).** [`push_refused.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/push_refused.go) separates non-fast-forward porcelain rejections (`[rejected] (fetch first|non-fast-forward|stale info)`) as `*refusedPushError`. On `*refusedPushError`, `healRefusedPush` fetches `<remote> <ref>` (`FETCH_HEAD`), acquires `land.lock` (`lockTrunkHistory`), replays `FETCH_HEAD..refs/heads/main` linearly via `git merge-tree --write-tree` and `git commit-tree`, pins `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` to the fetched tip (trusted by `CheckIdentities` in [`identity_history.go`](../../tools/tillr-land/land/identity_history.go)), resets `refs/heads/main` and the main checkout via `git reset --keep -q`, and calls `observeTrunk` to record `trunk_rewrites` by `git patch-id --stable` and carry `refs/tillr/green`. In `"tip"` mode `pushTrunk` runs again immediately; in `"green"` mode `afterGreen` leaves `<remote>` untouched until the next green advance.
3. **Remaining gaps on conflicts, force-pushes, and non-refusal failures.** Today a rebase conflict in `healRefusedPush` changes nothing and stops with `mover: operator` instead of dispatching conflict repair ([OQ-RP2](#OQ-RP2)). `pushCommand` does not update `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` on a normal successful push, `boundary.go` does not excuse `refs/tillr/published/` during concurrent runs, and `healRefusedPush` does not check whether the remote rewound past `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>`. Server-side `[remote rejected]` declines and [`hooks/pre-push`](../../hooks/pre-push) `patch_moves` holds are lumped into generic error strings.
4. **Misleading waiting rows and hidden accumulation on operator surfaces.** `recordPush` writes only `{action, detail, what}` on `drain.post-landing-action`. In [`waiting.go`](../../tools/tillr-view/waiting.go), `appendPostLandingWaiting` turns any non-empty `detail` — even one whose text says `mover: operator` — into one `post-landing:push` row with `Class: "waits on work"`, `Since: "—"`, and `Mover: "the next green advance"` (or `"the next relevant landing"` for `"tip"`). Because `Class` is `"waits on work"` and `Mover` names neither `owner` nor `operator`, `waitingActors` in [`waiting.ts`](../../tools/tillr-view/web/src/models/waiting.ts) counts every push hold under `next: drain`, [`ObservatoryHeaderBar.tsx`](../../tools/tillr-view/web/src/components/ObservatoryHeaderBar.tsx) shows no unpublished-trunk indicator, and an idle queue has no `tillr drain push` command after a hold clears.

## 2. Words and state ownership

Existing terms (`item`, `run`, `candidate`, `fast gate`, `heavy runner`, `green pointer`, `mechanic` / `trunk fixer`) follow [`tillr-vocabulary.md`](./tillr-vocabulary.md) and [`tillr-graph-is-the-path.md`](./tillr-graph-is-the-path.md). Six push-specific terms carry exact meanings here:

| Term | Meaning | What it is *not* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Refused push** *(coined here)* | A `git push` that reached the remote and returned a non-fast-forward rejection (`[rejected] (fetch first)`, `(non-fast-forward)`, or `(stale info)`) because the remote ref is not an ancestor of the pushed commit. | Not a local `pre-push` hook failure, git-broker denial, timeout, transport error, or server-side `[remote rejected]` policy decline. |
| **Failed push** *(coined here)* | Any non-zero or timed-out `git push` that is not a refused push — local hook exit, broker denial or timeout, SSH/HTTPS/network error, or `[remote rejected]` server hook failure. | Not evidence that the remote branch has new commits to rebase onto. |
| **Published pointer** *(coined here)* | Per-clone ref `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` recording the last commit OID this clone confirmed on `<remote>` `<ref>` (by a successful push or an accepted forward fetch). | Not `refs/remotes/<remote>/<branch>`, which `git fetch` overwrites before checking whether the remote rewound. |
| **Remote rebase** *(coined here)* | Replaying unpublished local commits onto the fetched remote tip $R$ in `tillr-drain` under `land.lock` (`healRefusedPush` for clean replays, or conflict-repair completion for conflicting ones; fast-forwarding straight to $R$ when local `main` has none), moving `refs/heads/main` and the main checkout via `git reset --keep -q`, and remapping recorded landing SHAs ([OQ-RP1](#OQ-RP1)). | Not a merge commit on `main`, not an unlocked `git pull` racing `tillr-land`, and never a `--force` push to the remote. |
| **Remote rewrite** *(coined here)* | A fetched remote tip $R$ that does not descend from this clone's published pointer $R_{\text{prev}}$ ($R_{\text{prev}} \not\preceq R$), meaning someone force-pushed the remote branch. | Not an ordinary forward push by another machine ($R_{\text{prev}} \preceq R$). |
| **Conflict repair** *(coined here)* | A deduplicated express `build` item (`sync-remote-<short(R)>`) that resolves textual rebase conflicts when replaying unpublished local commits onto a fetched forward remote tip $R$ in an isolated worktree, escalating obstacles like any other item ([OQ-RP2](#OQ-RP2)). | Not an unreviewed direct write to the main checkout, not a step that asks the owner first in the ordinary case, and not a license to edit files outside the conflict fence. |

### One writer per ref and record

| State | Single writer | Concurrency and boundary rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `FETCH_HEAD` and `refs/remotes/<remote>/<branch>` | `tillr-drain`'s single `pushAction` worker via targeted `git fetch` and `git push` | Ignored by worker `boundary.check` ([`tools/tillr-drain/README.md`](../../tools/tillr-drain/README.md)); serialized by `landingAction`'s single worker goroutine. |
| `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` | `tillr-drain`'s `pushAction` worker on every successful push and accepted forward fetch (`land.PublishedRefPrefix` in [`identity_history.go`](../../tools/tillr-land/land/identity_history.go)) | Added to `boundary.go`'s excused `refs/tillr/` prefixes alongside `refs/tillr/green` and `refs/tillr/trunk-history/*` so background pushes never convict a concurrent worker's boundary snapshot. |
| `refs/heads/main`, the main checkout, and `trunk_rewrites` | Serialized by `<git-common-dir>/land.lock`: [`tools/tillr-land`](../../tools/tillr-land/) for candidate squash landings, and `tillr-drain` (`lockTrunkHistory` + `git reset --keep -q` + `observeTrunk`) for remote rebases and external `git pull --rebase` absorption | Both candidate landings and remote rebases acquire `land.lock` before reading or moving `refs/heads/main`; `git reset --keep -q` refuses without changing any ref or file when the main checkout is off `main` or has local changes on a path touched by $T \to T'$, and SQLite `trunk_rewrites` updates run after releasing Git locks (`store.CheckIO`). |
| `refs/tillr/green` | `HeavyRunner` in [`heavy.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/heavy.go) for forward proof advances, plus `observeTrunk` carrying `refs/tillr/green` to `absorb.Pairs[green]` on patch-identical clean replays (`8fe514714`) | Never carried onto or past a conflict-repaired commit $C_k'$ (held behind $C_k'$ until `HeavyRunner` proves the repaired tip), and never left off `main`'s ancestry. |

## 3. Telling a refused push apart from a failed one

Stderr substring matching cannot separate a moved remote from other failures: a local [`hooks/pre-push`](../../hooks/pre-push) failure, a yolo git-broker rejection, and a GitHub branch-protection hook can all print the word `"rejected"` while the remote branch has not moved at all. Classification therefore uses two mechanical steps (`D-RP1`).

### 3.1 Step 1: Porcelain ref status separates transport and hook failures from non-fast-forward refusals

`pushCommand` (`pushGreen`, `pushTrunk`, and `tillr drain push`) runs `LC_ALL=C git push --porcelain -- <remote> <pushed>:<ref>`. Stdout's `<flag>\t<from>:<to>\t<summary>` table separates the outcomes cleanly before reading prose (the refused-push match landed in `8fe514714`; the remaining classes extend `pushCommand` and `recordPush` without altering clean replay):

| `--porcelain` stdout for `<ref>` | Exit | Diagnosis | Next step |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `<space>` (`<old>..<new>`) or `=` (`[up to date]`) | `0` | Published | Advance `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` to `<pushed>` (and when `pushGreen` skips via `errAlreadyPublished`, confirm `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` holds at least `<green>`) and clear any push warning. |
| `!` with `[rejected] (fetch first)`, `[rejected] (non-fast-forward)`, or `[rejected] (stale info)` | `1` | **Refused push (`*refusedPushError`, built in `8fe514714`)** | Run Step 2 (`git fetch` + local DAG classification in `healRefusedPush`) immediately in the same `pushAction` turn. |
| `!` with `[remote rejected]` (with or without `(<reason>)`) | `1` | **Failed push: `push-remote-declined`** (server hook, branch protection, secret scan) | Record `push-remote-declined` (`needs you`); do not fetch-and-rebase, because fast-forward was not why the server declined. |
| No porcelain line for `<ref>`, and an unacknowledged `patch_moves` hold is active in SQLite (`patchmoves`) | non-`0` | **Failed push: `push-patch-move-held`** ([`bin/patch-branches-publish`](../../bin/patch-branches-publish) refused in [`hooks/pre-push`](../../hooks/pre-push)) | Record `push-patch-move-held` (`needs you`), naming `tillr drain acknowledge-patch-move`. |
| No porcelain line for `<ref>` (other local hook failure, git-broker denial/timeout, auth, or network `exit 128`) | non-`0` or deadline | **Failed push: `push-failed`** | Record `push-failed` with stderr detail (`needs you` on broker/hook/auth failure; retryable via `tillr drain push` or the next push trigger). |

### 3.2 Step 2: Fetching the target ref classifies the exact commit graph

On a Step 1 `*refusedPushError`, `healRefusedPush` records the current trunk in `observeTrunk`, reads any prior `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` OID $R_{\text{prev}}$ (falling back to any existing `refs/remotes/<remote>/<branch>` OID when `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` has not been written yet), and runs (`8fe514714`):

```sh
git fetch -- <remote> <ref>
```

resolving `FETCH_HEAD^{commit}` as $R$ (`remoteTip`). In [`config/yolo-jail/config.toml`](../../config/yolo-jail/config.toml), `[allow.git]` sets `always = ["fetch"]` and `on_request = ["new_branches", "push"]`, so `git fetch` runs unattended inside the jail without popping an approval prompt. If `git fetch` itself fails, the turn records `push-failed` and changes nothing.

Once `git fetch` resolves $R$ (`FETCH_HEAD^{commit}`), `healRefusedPush` checks $R_{\text{prev}}$ and, under `land.lock`, compares $R$ against $T$ (`refs/heads/main^{commit}`):

| Local ancestry check after `fetch` | Meaning | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $R_{\text{prev}}$ exists and $R_{\text{prev}} \not\preceq R$ | **Remote rewrite (`remote-rewritten`):** someone force-pushed `<remote>` `<ref>`, dropping or replacing commits this clone already confirmed on the remote (`D-RP2`). | Stop without changing any ref or file and record `remote-rewritten` (`needs you`, `mover: operator`). Never auto-rebase: replaying local commits (which include $R_{\text{prev}}$) onto $R$ and pushing would re-publish the commits the force-push removed. |
| $R \preceq T$ (and $R_{\text{prev}}$ unset or $R_{\text{prev}} \preceq R$) | **Remote already under local trunk (`8fe514714`):** `remoteTip` is already an ancestor of `refs/heads/main`, so a moved remote ahead of the trunk does not explain the refusal. | Change nothing and fail naming `mover: operator` (keeping `8fe514714`'s `trunkAncestor(remoteTip, tip)` guard intact). |
| $T \prec R$ (`rev-list R..T` empty) | **Local behind remote:** the remote holds every local `main` commit plus new upstream commits (`replayed == 0`). | Pin `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` to $R$, fast-forward `refs/heads/main` and the main checkout to $R$ via `git reset --keep -q <R>`, and update `observeTrunk` (`8fe514714`). |
| $T \not\preceq R$, $R \not\preceq T$, and ($R_{\text{prev}}$ unset or $R_{\text{prev}} \preceq R$) | **Forward divergence:** another writer fast-forwarded `<remote>` to $R$ while local `main` advanced to $T$ from merge-base $B = \text{merge-base}(T, R)$. | Replay $R..T$ linearly onto $R$ ([Section 4](#4-rebasing-unpublished-commits-onto-the-moved-remote)), or if `git merge-tree --write-tree` conflicts, pin $R$ at `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` and dispatch conflict repair ([Section 6](#6-when-rebasing-onto-the-remote-conflicts)). |

## 4. Rebasing unpublished commits onto the moved remote

### 4.1 Linear trunk by rebasing unpublished commits onto the remote

On 2026-11-20 the owner ruled [OQ-RP1](#OQ-RP1) in favor of **Option B (rebase onto the remote)** rather than the proposal's merge-commit leaning: `refs/heads/main` stays linear, unpublished local commits are replayed onto the fetched remote tip, the main checkout is reset to the result, and recorded landing commit IDs are remapped.

When local `main` ($T$) and the fetched remote ($R$) have both advanced past their merge-base $B = \text{merge-base}(T, R)$ ($T \not\preceq R$ and $R \not\preceq T$), `healRefusedPush` in [`push_refused.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/push_refused.go) replays the unpublished local commits $C_1, \dots, C_n \in R..T$ ($= B..T$) onto $R$ as single-parent commits $B \to R_1 \to \dots \to R \to C_1' \to \dots \to T'$. That satisfies $R \preceq T'$ (so the next push to `<remote>` `<ref>` is a pure fast-forward with no `--force`) while dropping the pre-rebase tip $T$ from `main`'s ancestry ($T \not\preceq T'$).

Unexplained rewrites of `refs/heads/main` continue to halt landings under `ReasonTrunkRewritten` in [`trunk_history.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/trunk_history.go). Deliberate rebases preserve every local commit across $T \to T'$:

1. **Manual `git pull --rebase` (`98cc40ffe`) and clean `healRefusedPush` replay (`8fe514714`):** [`trunk_rewrite.go`](../../tools/tillr-drain/drain/trunk_rewrite.go) detects $T \to T'$ in `observeTrunk`, matches each commit in $B..T$ to a commit in $B..T'$ via `git patch-id --stable`, carries `refs/tillr/green` to `absorb.Pairs[green]`, pins $T$ at `refs/tillr/trunk-history/accepted/<T>`, and writes `trunk_rewrites(old_sha, new_sha, recorded_at, evidence)`. Commit `8fe514714`'s clean-replay path is kept as built and not rebuilt around a separate `tillr-land sync-remote` command.
2. **Direct `trunk_rewrites` recording when replay alters a commit's diff (`D-RP3`, `D-RP5`):** When conflict repair ([Section 6](#6-when-rebasing-onto-the-remote-conflicts)) replaces $C_k$ with a conflict-resolved commit $C_k'$ — or when shifted upstream context in $B..R$ or empty supersession ($C_i$'s exact tree change already in $R$) alters `git patch-id --stable` on an otherwise clean three-way merge — `git patch-id --stable` no longer matches every $C_i \mapsto C_i'$ pair ([Principle 2](./landing-principles.md#2-re-application-preserves-the-change-not-the-patch)). Because `tillr-drain` constructed each $C_i \mapsto C_i'$ pair directly under `land.lock`, it passes those known pairs into `observeTrunk` / `rewriteAbsorption` so `trunk_rewrites`, `refs/tillr/trunk-history/accepted/<T>`, and `trunk_history.observed_tip = T'` record cleanly without raising `trunk-rewritten`.

### 4.2 What `healRefusedPush` does under the landing lock

Under [OQ-RP1](#OQ-RP1) and `D-RP3`, `tillr-drain` serializes remote rebases with `tillr-land` by acquiring `<git-common-dir>/land.lock` (`lockTrunkHistory`) inside `healRefusedPush` (keeping `8fe514714`'s clean-replay flow intact):

1. **Sequential three-way replay onto $R$ (`8fe514714`, extended for supersession and conflict repair).** Under `land.lock`, `healRefusedPush` reads `refs/heads/main^{commit}` ($T$) and walks `git rev-list --reverse --topo-order --parents <R>..<T>` ($C_1, \dots, C_n$, empty when $T \prec R$):
   - Any multi-parent merge or root commit in $R..T$ refuses without changing anything (`mover: operator`, `8fe514714`).
   - Each single-parent commit $C_i$ runs `git merge-tree --write-tree --merge-base=<C_i^1> <P_{i-1}> <C_i>` (starting at $P_0 = R$).
   - If `merge-tree` exits `1` with `CONFLICT`, replay leaves `refs/heads/main`, `refs/tillr/green`, and the main checkout untouched, pins `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` at $R$, and dispatches conflict repair ([Section 6](#6-when-rebasing-onto-the-remote-conflicts)) rather than stopping on `mover: operator`.
   - If the merged tree equals $P_{i-1}$'s tree (an upstream commit in $B..R$ already applied the exact same change as $C_i$, [Principle 10](./landing-principles.md#10-supersession-is-a-verdict-not-a-stall)), replay skips writing an empty commit and maps $C_i \mapsto P_{i-1}$.
   - Otherwise `git commit-tree <tree_i> -p <P_{i-1}>` writes $C_i'$, preserving $C_i$'s commit message, author, and `GIT_AUTHOR_DATE` (`8fe514714`).
2. **Pinning the fetched upstream tip (`97d37524b`).** Before moving `refs/heads/main`, `healRefusedPush` updates `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` (`land.PublishedRefPrefix`) to $R$ so `CheckIdentities` trusts commits that arrived from the remote while still checking the replayed commits above $R$ ([Section 4.3](#43-trusting-commit-identities-already-on-the-upstream-branch)).
3. **Guarded reset of the main checkout (`git reset --keep -q`, `8fe514714`).** Still holding `land.lock`, `healRefusedPush` verifies that the main checkout's `HEAD` symbolic-ref is `refs/heads/main` and that `refs/heads/main` still equals $T$, then runs `git reset --keep -q <T'>`:
   - **Dirty main-checkout and detached-HEAD protection:** `git reset --keep` refuses without changing any ref or file whenever any path differing between $T$ and $T'$ has local uncommitted modifications in the main checkout (while keeping unrelated dirty files intact), and checking `HEAD` refuses when the main checkout is off `main`. On either refusal, `tillr-drain` records `remote-sync-worktree-dirty` (`needs you`, `mover: operator`).
4. **Remapping recorded landing commit IDs, markers, and the green pointer.** After releasing `land.lock`, `observeTrunk` records $T \to T'$:
   - On a patch-identical clean replay (`8fe514714`), `proveRewrite` matches $C_i \mapsto C_i'$ by `git patch-id --stable`, writes `trunk_rewrites` and `drain.trunk-rewrite-absorbed`, pins `refs/tillr/trunk-history/accepted/<T>`, updates `trunk_history.observed_tip = T'` (`lost_tip = ''`), and carries `refs/tillr/green` to `absorb.Pairs[green]`.
   - When a replayed commit's diff changed (conflict repair under `D-RP5`, shifted adjacent context in $B..R$, or empty supersession), `observeTrunk` uses the known $C_i \mapsto C_i'$ map recorded by the replay instead of failing on `proveRewrite`, and on conflict repair holds `refs/tillr/green` strictly behind the repaired commit $C_k'$ ([Section 5](#5-proving-the-rebased-trunk-before-pushing)).
   - Live `refs/tillr/landed/<run_id>` markers pointing at $C_i \in B..T$ update to $C_i'$, and remaining readers of `landings.trunk_after` resolve through `trunk_rewrites` (`RewriteResolve` / `RewriteAliases`), keeping crash recovery, ref cleanup, lane-base blocker checks, comment reply delivery, `firstRed` bisection, and `auto_revert` intact across the rebase.

### 4.3 Trusting commit identities already on the upstream branch

When `main` moves from $T$ to $T'$ (or fast-forwards from $T$ to $R$), `CheckIdentities` in [`identity_history.go`](../../tools/tillr-land/land/identity_history.go) walks `old..tip` and compares every commit against this machine's `LandingIdentity()`. Because this repository deploys distinct git emails across work and personal machines (`tag-work` vs `tag-personal`), [`git-identity.test.py`](../../tests/git-identity.test.py) bounds its check at `merge-base HEAD origin/main` rather than re-judging published remote commits.

Per the owner's 2026-11-20 ruling on [OQ-RP3](#OQ-RP3) (**Option A**), built in `97d37524b`, commits reachable from `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` (`land.PublishedRefPrefix`) are trusted by `CheckIdentities` without requiring `tillr drain accept-identity`:

- `healRefusedPush` pins the fetched tip $R$ at `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>`, and `CheckIdentities` skips any commit that is an ancestor of any ref under `refs/tillr/published/` (`onPublished`), because any commit already on `<remote>` `<ref>` passed push credentials on the machine that published it.
- Local commits replayed in $R..T'$ are above `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` and remain subject to `CheckIdentities`; if an ad-hoc local commit $C_i \in B..T$ had already been accepted in `trunk_identity_acceptances`, rebasing carries that acceptance forward to $C_i'$ so replaying it onto $R$ does not re-trigger `trunk-foreign-identity`.

## 5. Proving the rebased trunk before pushing

How proof and publication follow a remote rebase (`D-RP4`) keeps `8fe514714`'s clean-replay behavior intact while proving conflict-repaired commits before publication ([Principle 3](./landing-principles.md#3-gate-what-will-be-the-tip-once)):

1. **Clean replay (`healRefusedPush`, built in `8fe514714` and kept as built):**
   - `observeTrunk` maps each replaced commit into `trunk_rewrites` and carries `refs/tillr/green` to `absorb.Pairs[green]` when `green` is among the patch-matched replayed commits (`TestRefusedPushRebasesOntoTheMovedRemote`).
   - **When `push.what = "green"`:** `afterGreen` does **not** re-push immediately on the refused-push turn (`TestRefusedGreenPushHealsThroughTheAction`), recording `remote-sync-proving` (`Class: "waits on work"`). When `refs/tillr/green < T'` (including when $T \prec R$), `HeavyRunner` proves `green..T'` on `main`: on pass, `refs/tillr/green` advances to $T'$ (triggering `roll.command` if $B..R$ changed `roll.Paths`) and `afterGreen` fast-forwards `<remote>` `<ref>` and advances `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>`; on fail, `HeavyRunner` bisects `green..T'` via `firstRed` (resolving rebased local landings through `trunk_rewrites`, never auto-reverting upstream commits in $B..R$) and auto-reverts or files `fixer-main-<sha12>`. When `refs/tillr/green` was already at $T$ and carried to $T'$ on an idle queue, `tillr drain push` (or the next push trigger) fast-forwards `<remote>` `<ref>` to `refs/tillr/green`.
   - **When `push.what = "tip"`:** `afterLandingCandidate` resets `main` to $T'$ and immediately re-runs `pushTrunk` once (`8fe514714`), advancing `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` on success.
2. **Conflict repair ([OQ-RP2](#OQ-RP2), `D-RP5`):**
   - Because repaired commit $C_k'$ is newly authored to reconcile conflicting changes rather than a clean patch-identical replay, $C_k'$ passes the repair item's gate (and review when configured) in its lane before `main` moves.
   - When landing completes the chain $C_1'..\,C_k'..\,T_{\text{now}}'$ under `land.lock`, `refs/tillr/green` is **not** carried onto or past $C_k'$ (it stays at $G'$ when $G$ preceded $C_k$, or $\min(G, B)$ otherwise, keeping `refs/tillr/green` a strict ancestor of $T_{\text{now}}'$). In `"green"` mode, `tillr-drain` records `remote-sync-proving` and wakes `HeavyRunner`, which must advance `refs/tillr/green` to or past $T_{\text{now}}'$ before `pushGreen` publishes; in `"tip"` mode, `pushTrunk` publishes $T_{\text{now}}'$ once the gated repair resets `main`.

## 6. When rebasing onto the remote conflicts

When replaying $C_1, \dots, C_n \in R..T$ onto $R$ in `healRefusedPush` hits a `git merge-tree --write-tree` exit `1` on commit $C_k$, neither `refs/heads/main`, `refs/tillr/green`, nor the main checkout is touched, and no conflict markers are written to the main checkout. Neither side can simply be sent back to an unlanded lane: $R$ is already published on `<remote>`, and $R..T$ is already committed on local `main`.

Per the owner's 2026-11-20 ruling on [OQ-RP2](#OQ-RP2), instead of stopping with `mover: operator`, the drain repairs rebase conflicts automatically through a fenced repair worker and escalates obstacles the same way any other item's obstacles escalate, with no step that involves the owner in the ordinary case:

1. **Deduplicated express repair item.** With no human prompt beforehand, `healRefusedPush` pins `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` at $R$ and files (or attaches to) at most one open repair item per remote tip $R$ (`sync-remote-<short(R)>`, 12-hex prefix of $R$, express priority, `build` seat, routed on a free express/ordinary lane or a dedicated repair desk when all lanes hold retained candidates). Its initial fence (`allowed_paths`) is the conflicting path set reported by `git merge-tree` for $C_k$ onto $P_{k-1}$ (where $P_{k-1}$ is $C_{k-1}'$, or $R$ when $k = 1$; excluding `GateCodePaths`), widened on a `fence` obstacle through `drain.fixer-fence-widened`.
2. **Brief and linear staging onto $P_{k-1}$.** The lane stages at $P_{k-1}$ ($R \preceq P_{k-1}$, pinned under `refs/tillr/runs/` so GC retains it) and receives the conflicting file paths, conflict output, commit messages on $B..R$ and $C_k$, and any item summary recorded in `landings` for $C_k$. The worker resolves the conflict and commits a single linear commit $C_k'$ on top of $P_{k-1}$, keeping $C_k$'s subject/intent and zero merge parents.
3. **Locked non-conflicting tree check (`D-RP5`).** Before running the gate, the drain compares the worker's commit $C_k'$ against the mechanical three-way merge of $(P_{k-1}, C_k)$ over merge-base $C_k^1$ on every path **outside** the item's fence: every unfenced path in $C_k'$'s tree must match the exact blob OID produced by `merge-tree` on $(P_{k-1}, C_k)$. A worker therefore cannot silently drop an unrelated file brought in by $R$ or revert an earlier rebased commit in $R..P_{k-1}$.
4. **Gate, review, linear rebase completion, and heavy proof.** $C_k'$ runs through the gate (and code review when configured). At landing time in `tillr-drain` under `land.lock` (`lockTrunkHistory`), the drain replays any remaining commits $C_{k+1}..\,T_{\text{now}}$ (including any concurrent local landings $T_0..\,T_{\text{now}}$ that arrived while the worker ran) linearly atop $C_k'$ via `git merge-tree --write-tree` and `git commit-tree` — never writing a merge commit. If a later commit $C_m$ ($m > k$) also conflicts, the drain advances `sync-remote-<short(R)>`'s staged base to $C_{m-1}'$ with $C_m$'s conflict paths for the next step without moving `main`. Once the full chain reaches $T_{\text{now}}'$ cleanly, the drain checks `HEAD == refs/heads/main`, resets `refs/heads/main` and the main checkout from $T_{\text{now}}$ to $T_{\text{now}}'$ via `git reset --keep -q <T_now'>` (holding as `remote-sync-worktree-dirty` if clashing uncommitted edits exist), records every $C_i \mapsto C_i'$ pair (including $C_k \mapsto C_k'$) directly in `trunk_rewrites` via `observeTrunk`, and holds `refs/tillr/green` strictly behind $C_k'$ so in `"green"` mode `HeavyRunner` proves $T_{\text{now}}'$ before `pushGreen` publishes it.
5. **Standard obstacle escalation.** Obstacles reported by the repair worker (`fence`, `definition`, `tooling`, `seat`, `owner`) follow the same generated-repair obstacle routing and `repeatedEnding` loop guard as trunk fixers: `fence` widens `allowed_paths` (escalating to `owner` only if `GateCodePaths` is requested), `seat` escalates to a stronger model or design seat, and an unresolvable obstacle (`owner`, `definition`, `tooling`) or repeated identical failure transitions `remote-sync-conflict` to `needs you` naming `owner` or `operator` as mover.

Path-disjoint local items continue running in their lanes while conflict repair runs; they are not killed or blamed ([Principle 14](./landing-principles.md#14-convict-a-run-on-what-it-did-not-on-what-happened-while-it-ran)).

## 7. Showing the state and its mover while it lasts

To satisfy [Principle 7](./landing-principles.md#7-a-surface-that-hides-parked-work-is-a-false-report) and [Principle 13](./landing-principles.md#13-a-held-state-names-a-mover-that-can-still-act), `drain.post-landing-action` (or a dedicated `push_state` projection keyed by `action = "push"`) records a structured `kind` alongside `detail`, `remote_tip`, `since_ts` (preserved across updates until `push-ok` clears it), and unpublished counts (`D-RP6`).

### 7.1 Waiting rows and named movers

`appendPostLandingWaiting` in [`waiting.go`](../../tools/tillr-view/waiting.go) maps each non-`ok` push state to the class and mover prefix that `waitingActors` in [`waiting.ts`](../../tools/tillr-view/web/src/models/waiting.ts) routes accurately (`needs you` with `owner; …` or `operator; …` whenever human action is required, `waits on work` only while an active drain step is actually advancing publication):

| State `kind` | `Class` | `Mover` | What `Why` states (alongside real `Since`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `remote-sync-proving` | `waits on work` | `the heavy runner; prove rebased tip <short(T')> before pushing` | Rebased `<n>` local commit(s) onto remote `<short(R)>` at `<short(T')>`; awaiting heavy proof before pushing. |
| `remote-sync-conflict` (repair active) | `waits on work` | `<sync-item-id>; resolve rebase conflict onto <remote>/<branch> (<short(R)>)` | Rebasing onto `<short(R)>` conflicted on `<paths>`; `<k>` proven / `<n>` total local commits unpublished while repair runs. |
| `remote-sync-conflict` (escalated) | `needs you` | `owner; …` (on owner obstacle) or `operator; rebase main onto <remote>/<branch> (<short(R)>)` | Conflict repair for rebase onto `<short(R)>` escalated (`<reason>`) on `<paths>`; `<k>` proven / `<n>` total local commits unpublished. |
| `remote-sync-worktree-dirty` | `needs you` | `operator; check out main or commit, stash, or restore clashing paths in the main checkout` | Cannot reset `main` to rebased `<short(T')>` because the main checkout is off `main` or has uncommitted edits on clashing paths. |
| `remote-rewritten` | `needs you` | `operator; reconcile force-pushed <remote>/<branch> (<short(R_prev)> -> <short(R)>)` | Remote rewound or rewrote `<short(R_prev)>` to `<short(R)>`; autopush stopped to avoid re-publishing dropped commits. |
| `push-patch-move-held` | `needs you` | `operator; run tillr drain acknowledge-patch-move after checking the fork rewrite` | [`hooks/pre-push`](../../hooks/pre-push) held publication on an unacknowledged patch-branch move; `<k>` proven commits unpublished. |
| `push-remote-declined` | `needs you` | `operator; inspect remote rejection on <remote> <ref> and run tillr drain push` | Remote server declined `<short(P)>`: `<detail>`; `<k>` proven commits unpublished. |
| `push-failed` | `needs you` | `operator; check git-broker approval or remote access, or run tillr drain push` | Push of `<short(P)>` did not reach a ref update (`<detail>`); `<k>` proven commits unpublished (also re-attempted on the next push trigger). |

### 7.2 Header badge and idle-queue recovery

1. **Observatory header and `tillr view status`.** A drained queue with `0 running` must not look finished while proven commits sit unpublished locally. Status payloads add a `push` summary (`published_sha`, `unpublished_green_count`, `unpublished_main_count`, `state_kind`, `mover`, `since`), and [`ObservatoryHeaderBar.tsx`](../../tools/tillr-view/web/src/components/ObservatoryHeaderBar.tsx) renders an unpublished pill whenever `push.what != "none"` and either `state_kind` is non-empty or proven commits remain ahead of `published_sha` after the push worker goes idle.
2. **Publishing after a hold clears on an idle queue (`tillr drain push`).** Relying solely on `"the next green advance"` strands publication when the queue has already finished its last item and the operator subsequently restores Wi-Fi, approves the git broker, resolves a dirty main checkout, or runs `tillr drain acknowledge-patch-move`. Adding an explicit `tillr drain push` command — and having `acknowledge-patch-move` and dirty-checkout clearance enqueue `pushAction` when `green` (or `main` in `"tip"` mode) is ahead of `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` — publishes the parked commits immediately without needing a dummy landing or loop restart.

## 8. Invariants, non-goals, and risks

### 8.1 What must never happen

- **No local commit is silently dropped on rebase, and `main` stays linear:** remote rebases write zero merge commits on `refs/heads/main`; every unpublished local commit in $B..T$ is replayed onto $R$ and mapped in `trunk_rewrites` (with $T$ pinned at `refs/tillr/trunk-history/accepted/<T>`, or superseded only when its exact tree change is already in $R$), while any unmapped external rewrite still halts on `trunk-rewritten`.
- **A push never forces the remote:** `git push` never passes `+`, `--force`, or `--force-with-lease` on `<ref>`.
- **A remote force-push is never silently undone:** when $R_{\text{prev}} \not\preceq R$, the drain never rebases local commits (which include $R_{\text{prev}}$) onto $R$ and pushes them back onto the remote.
- **Conflict-repaired commits never publish unproven in `"green"` mode:** while `8fe514714`'s clean patch-identical replay carries `refs/tillr/green` via `observeTrunk` without pushing immediately on the refused turn, conflict-repair landing holds `refs/tillr/green` strictly behind the repaired commit $C_k'$ until `HeavyRunner` advances `refs/tillr/green` to or past $T_{\text{now}}'$.
- **Uncommitted edits in the main checkout are never overwritten:** `git reset --keep -q` (requiring `HEAD == refs/heads/main`) refuses clashing paths without moving any ref or file, waiting as `remote-sync-worktree-dirty`.
- **Cross-machine pushes on the upstream branch never freeze local landings on foreign identity**, while local commits remain checked ([OQ-RP3](#OQ-RP3), built in `97d37524b`).
- **Non-diverged failures never claim `"the next green advance"` as their mover** when a green advance cannot clear them.

### 8.2 What this does not do

- Does not poll the remote on a timer when no push is due; remote state is read only when a push is refused or `tillr drain push` is invoked.
- Does not change how fork `patch/<name>` branches are published or guarded by [`bin/patch-branches-publish`](../../bin/patch-branches-publish); it surfaces `patch_moves` holds truthfully instead of masking them as `"waits on work"`.
- Does not rebase branches other than the configured trunk (`refs/heads/main` onto its configured `<remote>` `<ref>`).

### 8.3 Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- |
| Rebase or conflict repair introduces a semantic build or test failure between $B..R$ and replayed $B..T$. | On clean replay in `"green"` mode, `afterGreen` does not immediately re-push (`8fe514714`) and `HeavyRunner` proves any `green..T'` range (resolving rebased landings through `trunk_rewrites` via `firstRed` to auto-revert or file `fixer-main-<sha12>`); on conflict repair, `C_k'` passes the repair item's gate before landing and `refs/tillr/green` stays behind `C_k'` until `HeavyRunner` proves $T_{\text{now}}'$. |
| Operator force-pushes `<remote>` `<ref>` to drop a bad commit, and a running drain rebases local history containing that commit onto $R$ and re-pushes it. | `refs/tillr/published/<remote>/<branch>` checks $R_{\text{prev}} \preceq R$ on every refused-push fetch (`D-RP2`); $R_{\text{prev}} \not\preceq R$ stops autopushing as `remote-rewritten` (`needs you`). |
| Conflict-repair worker accidentally reverts or drops non-conflicting files from $R$ or earlier local commits. | `D-RP5` locks every path outside the item's fence in repaired commit $C_k'$ to the exact blob OID of the mechanical three-way merge of $(P_{k-1}, C_k)$ over $C_k^1$ before the gate runs. |
| Frequent remote pushes arrive faster than `HeavyRunner` finishes proving each rebase. | `landingAction`'s single replaceable pending slot coalesces triggers, and `HeavyRunner` proves the latest `main` tip in batch rather than proving intermediate rebases one by one. |

### 8.4 What done looks like

1. Pushing a non-conflicting commit to `<remote>` `<ref>` from another clone (including from another machine with a different git email) while local landings are unpublished causes the next autopush to detect `[rejected] (fetch first)`, fetch $R$, replay $B..T$ linearly onto $R$ under `land.lock`, reset the main checkout, remap landing SHAs in `trunk_rewrites`, prove the rebased tip, and fast-forward `<remote>` `<ref>` with zero manual intervention and zero merge commits.
2. Pushing a conflicting commit to `<remote>` `<ref>` dispatches one fenced `sync-remote-<short(R)>` repair item that resolves the conflict linearly onto $R$, passes gate, review, and heavy proof, and escalates through standard obstacle routing only when an obstacle or repeated failure holds it.
3. Force-pushing `<remote>` `<ref>` backwards stops autopush immediately as `remote-rewritten` (`Class: "needs you"`) without rebasing and re-publishing the dropped commit onto the remote.
4. A local `pre-push` failure, `[remote rejected]` decline, or broker denial records its distinct state and real `needs you` mover in `tillr view waiting` and the Observatory header, with accurate `Since` and unpublished commit counts, and `tillr drain push` publishes immediately once the operator clears the cause.
